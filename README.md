## 三步就能用 Three steps

1. **电脑右键 Right-click** —— 选中文件或文件夹，右键选择快传。Select files or folders, then right-click to share.
2. **弹出二维码 QR code** —— 手机上扫码。Scan it with your phone.
3. **手机查看或下载 View or download** —— 浏览器直接打开，微信里也能保存、转发；还可反向上传文件到电脑。Open in your browser, save and forward in WeChat, or upload files back to your computer.

- 支持手机上传，可开关「允许手机上传」 — Phone-to-PC upload, toggleable "Allow upload"
- Windows 资源管理器 / macOS Finder 右键菜单集成 — Integrated into Windows Explorer / macOS Finder context menu
- 免安装 App，手机端零安装 — No app needed on your phone

# RightSend

> File sync at LAN speed.

Right-click to share files, get a QR code, and scan it with your phone to view or download — you can even upload files from your phone back to your PC. Direct over your LAN, with no mobile data used.

[中文](#右键快传-rightsend) ↑

**Website：** https://epaii.github.io/rightsend/

## Download

| Platform | Installer |
|---|---|
| Windows | [RightSend-Setup-1.0.0.exe](https://github.com/epaii/rightsend/releases/latest/download/RightSend-Setup-1.0.0.exe) |
| macOS (Universal: Intel / Apple Silicon) | [RightSend-1.0.0.pkg](https://github.com/epaii/rightsend/releases/latest/download/RightSend-1.0.0.pkg) |

All releases: [Releases](https://github.com/epaii/rightsend/releases)

> The macOS installer is unsigned. If blocked by Gatekeeper, right-click → Open, or run `xattr -cr /path/to/RightSend-1.0.0.pkg` before installing.

## Three steps

1. **Right-click** — select files or folders, then choose RightSend from the context menu.
2. **QR code appears** — scan it with your phone.
3. **View or download** — open in your mobile browser, save and forward in WeChat, or upload files back to your PC.

## Features

- Direct LAN transfer at full WiFi speed — no mobile data
- Open and download in your browser; save and forward in WeChat; videos play while downloading
- Cloud-drive-style browsing: search, sort, preview, download originals
- Phone-to-PC upload with a toggleable "Allow upload" switch
- Integrated into Windows Explorer / macOS Finder context menus
- No app installation needed on your phone
